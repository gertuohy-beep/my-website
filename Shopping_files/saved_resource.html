How to Use

Select Currency: Choose your preferred currency from the dropdown menu at the top.

Enter Who Paid the Bill: Type the name of the person who paid the full bill. This is the person who will be refunded by the others.

How many people in total? Select or enter how many people are sharing the bill (including the payer).

Enter Everyone’s Names: Enter the names of all the other people participating in the split.

Add Items from the Shopping Bill: Enter the original Item Cost. If there is a discount, enter it in the Item Discount field. Tick the checkboxes of people who should share that item.

Apply Store Vouchers: Enter any general store vouchers or discounts in the Store Voucher section. It will automatically apply an even deduction to everyone on the bill.

Track Total Cost Balance: Look at the dark blue running total card. It continually computes the net bill value after factoring in item costs, discounts, and store vouchers so you can verify it directly against your actual shop receipt total before splitting.

Make Changes if Needed: Click the red trash icon to delete any incorrect item or voucher entry.

Finish and Calculate: Click “Calculate Final Split” to see exactly how much each person owes the payer.
